# AOIFE KANE — CELTIC BATTLE-POP STUDIO ALBUM
## Master Album Tracklist (Tracks 01–10)

Every track is engineered strictly in the **Celtic battle-pop and Irish folk fusion** style of **Hold the Line**. Each track shares the exact core instrumentation, 118–122 BPM danceable tempo, Dorian mode harmony, driving bodhrán/kick/snare, and belted stadium hooks, while giving each song a unique opening instrument cue in the first 5 seconds.
