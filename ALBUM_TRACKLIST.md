# AOIFE KANE — DEBUT STUDIO ALBUM
## "NO BORROWED CROWN"
**Artist:** Aoife Kane  
**Core Identity:** Irish Celtic Battle-Pop & Modern Folk Fusion. Female alto vocals with a magnetic Irish lilt, slight vocal rasp, effortless stadium belting power, and fierce grounded energy.  
**Album Design Philosophy:** Every song has an unmistakably distinct intro, tempo, key, and instrumentation so the album never falls into repetitive formula.
